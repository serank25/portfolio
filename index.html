Warehouse Space Sharer

A marketplace that makes it easy for small businesses across India to find — and trust — flexible, verified warehouse space nearby.

Users

SMEs, D2C brands, and warehouse owners across Tier I–III Indian cities with unpredictable or seasonal storage needs.

Role

End-to-end product design — user research, interaction design, prototyping, and usability validation.

Tools

Figma, Stitch (AI design), HTML/CSS prototype, Google Slides for journey mapping.

Design Decisions

  • Role-based entry: "List Space" vs "Find Space" paths from the start, so each user type sees only what's relevant
  • Requirement wizard: A short guided flow instead of forcing users to browse — the product asks what you need before showing options
  • Comparable listing cards: Cards surface operational signals (security, equipment, certifications) alongside size and price
  • Assisted onboarding: Supply-side users (warehouse owners) get a guided listing flow to reduce drop-off and incomplete profiles
